Three moves. One waiting card.

Focus Resume Card is built for the moment before attention moves elsewhere—not for organizing the rest of your life.

  1. 1

    Mark the place

    The extension brings the current URL and any selected text. Hide either the title or selection before saving.

  2. 2

    Name the next action

    Write 5–12 words: a physical move small enough to start without replanning.

  3. 3

    Resume one tab

    Open the extension later. Your card—not a task list—appears first and restores the saved page.
